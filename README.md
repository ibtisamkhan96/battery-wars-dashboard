# Battery Wars: Who Powers the EV Revolution - 2025 Dashboard

Interactive dashboard on the global EV battery market, full-year 2025: maker rankings (CATL, BYD, LG, CALB, Gotion, SK On, Panasonic, EVE, Samsung SDI, Svolt), country concentration, the LFP vs nickel chemistry shift, and the solid-state race.

Part 2 of the Tech Wars dashboard series (Part 1: EV Wars USA vs China).

## Highlights

- Global installations hit 1,187 GWh in 2025, up 31.7%
- - CATL alone: 464.7 GWh, a 39.2% share - more than the next five makers combined
  - - Chinese makers in the top 10 hold ~70% of the world market; the Korean trio fell from 18.7% to 15.4%
    - - LFP chemistry overtook nickel globally for the first time (~50% share; 79% in China, 5% in North America)
